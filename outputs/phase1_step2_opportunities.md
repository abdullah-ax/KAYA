## Option A: WhatsApp IELTS speaking practice pack

- Problem: Students can't afford EGP 9,000 courses; need practice at home.
- Evidence: https://ielts.idp.com/egypt/prepare/article-free-speaking-practice-questions, https://ieltsbiz.com/ielts-fees/egypt, https://www.englishverseeg.com/knowledge/ielts-course-cost-egypt-2026-prices-guide/
- Who has it: IELTS test takers in Alexandria, Egypt, especially WhatsApp group members and language centre referrals
- Founder edge: 4 years coaching, band 8.5 score, makes short practice videos on phone
- First 10 people to ask: First 10 students record 3 answers, get checklist and model answer via WhatsApp
- Cost to test this week: EGP 500 for WhatsApp broadcast and simple template
- Biggest risk: Low engagement; students used to free resources
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 5

## Option B: Recorded speaking feedback pack with model answers

- Problem: No cheap way for students to get feedback on recorded IELTS speaking answers.
- Evidence: https://ielts.idp.com/egypt/prepare/speaking, https://ieltspeaking.com/guides/practice-ielts-speaking-alone-at-home.html, https://jurassicenglish.com/ielts-speaking-online/egypt
- Who has it: University-bound IELTS candidates in Egypt needing band 6.5 or 7
- Founder edge: Personal band 8.5, 4 years coaching, mobile video creation skill
- First 10 people to ask: First 10 students submit 2 recordings, receive checklist and model answer via WhatsApp
- Cost to test this week: EGP 1,000 for form setup and WhatsApp coordination
- Biggest risk: Feedback workload may exceed 10 hours per week; students reluctant to record
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 4

## Option C: Language centre practice pack for Smouha referrals

- Problem: 2 language centres refer students monthly; no standardized cheap practice material.
- Evidence: https://ielts.org/receiving-organisations/idp-education-alexandria, https://bandladder.com/study-abroad/ielts-for-egypt, https://ielts.idp.com/egypt/prepare/article/ielts-material

- Cost to test this week: EGP 1,500 for PDF pack and WhatsApp group setup
