# Opportunities (Phase 1, step 2)

2-3 options. The founder picks one and says why.

## Option A: WhatsApp speaking practice pack

- Problem: Alexandria IELTS students lack daily speaking practice at home.
- Evidence: https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/prepare/ielts-free-practice-mock-tests/academic/speaking, https://lingoleap.ai/blog/speaking-practice-tools-ielts
- Who has it: IELTS test takers in Alexandria needing band 6.5 or 7 for university admission.
- Founder edge: Mariam coached 300+ students since 2021, scored band 8.5, and makes practice videos on her phone.
- First 10 people to ask: Mariam records 10 speaking prompts, sends via WhatsApp, students record 2-minute answers, get 3-item checklist to self-score.
- Cost to test this week: EGP 1,500 for WhatsApp broadcast setup and simple checklist design; within the 2,000 EGP limit.
- Biggest risk: Risk of 30% non-response if students don't record answers; mitigated by daily reminders and small incentive for first 20 respondents.
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 4

## Option B: Video library of model answers and practice questions

- Problem: IELTS test takers in Egypt have no affordable source for short model answers they can compare their recordings against.
- Evidence: https://oxfordtefl.com/blog/helping-students-prepare-for-the-ielts-speaking-test-a-task-based-approach/, https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/
- Who has it: IELTS candidates in Egypt needing band 6.5 or 7 for work or university abroad.
- Founder edge: Mariam makes short practice videos for her students on her phone and has 4 years of coaching experience.
- First 10 people to ask: Mariam uploads 10 model answer videos; students record answers, watch model, and use 4-item checklist.
- Cost to test this week: EGP 800 for video recording, editing, and hosting on a free platform; within the 2,000 EGP budget.
- Biggest risk: Risk of low video views; mitigated by sharing via existing WhatsApp group and language centres.
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 4

## Option C: Partnership with Smouha language centres for bundled practice

- Problem: Two language centres in Smouha send 20+ IELTS students monthly to Mariam; these students have no structured speaking practice between centre classes.
- Evidence: https://ihworld.com/ih-journal/issues/issue-54/ielts-speaking-exam-preparation-activities/, https://www.esl-lounge.com/student/ielts-speaking.php
- Who has it: IELTS students referred by Smouha language centres, needing band 6.5 or 7 for admission.
- Founder edge: Mariam already has access to 420 students via WhatsApp and 2 language centres in Smouha.
- First 10 people to ask: Mariam offers the practice pack to centres at EGP 50 per student; centres promote it to their 20 monthly students.
- Cost to test this week: EGP 1,000 for printable checklist design and centre outreach; within the 2,000 EGP limit.
- Biggest risk: Risk of centres declining; mitigated by proposing a low-cost pilot with first 10 students.
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 4
