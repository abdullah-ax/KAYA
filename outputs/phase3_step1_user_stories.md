# User stories (Phase 3, step 1)

Opportunity: WhatsApp IELTS speaking practice pack

Drafted by the intern from your profile, your Lean Canvas and your sign-up page. Hours are the model's guess, so check them against your week.

## Story 1

As a Layla, a 24-year-old engineering graduate in Alexandria preparing for IELTS speaking, I want record three speaking answers at home and share them via WhatsApp voice note, so that practice speaking daily without spending EGP 9,000 on a course.

- Priority: must
- Time (model guess): 2 hours
- Acceptance criteria:
  - Given Layla has the WhatsApp template, when she records three answers, then she receives a checklist and model answer via broadcast
  - Given the model answer, when she compares her response, then she writes two notes on how to improve
- Smallest way to deliver it first: Founder sends a WhatsApp voice note with a practice question; students reply with voice notes.
- Backed by: https://ielts.idp.com/egypt/prepare/article-free-speaking-practice-questions
