# Opportunities (Phase 1, step 2)

2-3 options. The founder picks one and says why.

## Option A: Student deadline assistant with WhatsApp reminders

- Problem: University students miss assignment deadlines because they forget small tasks and use separate calendars.
- Evidence: https://engineeringanduniversity.wordpress.com/2017/01/06/student-assignments-missed-deadlines-and-the-planning-fallacy/, https://summer.harvard.edu/blog/8-time-management-tips-for-students/
- Who has it: Cairo university students
- Founder edge: Founder likes trying new apps and values simplicity; can build a quick version.
- First 10 people to ask: 10 Cairo university students who test daily task reminders for one month
- Cost to test this week: EGP 0 (free WhatsApp messages)
- Biggest risk: Students may ignore notifications or drop out after two weeks
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 3

## Option B: Office worker daily planner with calendar integration

- Problem: Office workers forget tasks because their calendars and to-do lists are in different apps.
- Evidence: https://www.any.do/blog/team-reminders-how-to-keep-your-team-on-track/, https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/microsoft-to-do-list-app
- Who has it: Cairo office workers
- Founder edge: Founder wants to help office workers remember tasks.
- First 10 people to ask: 10 Cairo office workers who try the app for two weeks and rate it
- Cost to test this week: EGP 0 (use free Microsoft To Do and Google Calendar)
- Biggest risk: Workers may resist adding another app to their already busy day
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 3

## Option C: General productivity seeker task prioritizer

- Problem: People who want to get more done feel overwhelmed by too many productivity apps and no clear daily plan.
- Evidence: https://habi.app/insights/best-productivity-apps/, https://buffer.com/resources/productivity-tools/
- Who has it: General productivity seekers in Egypt
- Founder edge: Founder's interest in new apps helps test which tools make using many apps easier.
- First 10 people to ask: 10 general users who use the app for one week and track task completion
- Cost to test this week: EGP 0 (free app stores)
- Biggest risk: Users may uninstall if the app does not show quick value
- Founder fit (model estimate, 1-5): 3
