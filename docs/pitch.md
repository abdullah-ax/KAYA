# Boardroom pitch

## 1. Problem
First-time founders start with an idea and no one to push back. They build for months before anyone pays, and the tools that help cost money they don't have. ASSUMPTION: from our own experience and early conversations, not yet measured.

## 2. Who it is for
A first-time founder with a skill and a group of people they can reach today: a tutor with a WhatsApp group, a designer with a client list. Egypt first, in English or Arabic.

## 3. What it does
A team of small AI agents takes the founder through six stages: Ideation, Validation, Build, Commercial setup, Sell and get paid, Operate and learn.
- It learns who the founder is first, then looks for problems that fit them, with a source for every claim or the word ASSUMPTION.
- Each stage ends at a gate. The system recommends go, repeat, pivot or stop, and the founder picks an option and says why. A bare "yes" never counts.
- After each passed stage, the founder moves on or keeps talking with the co-founder.
- It says stop when the evidence is weak, for example when the founder names no group they can reach.

## 4. Why open beats closed
- Every prompt, gate and threshold is a plain file anyone can read, test and change.
- It runs on a free open-weight model on the founder's laptop, so ideas and answers stay private and the cost per run is $0.00.
- Anyone can fork it for their own market, language or method.

## 5. What it will not do
It will not promise income, write fake reviews or sign-ups, pose as anyone, move or hold money, or publish anything without the founder's approval. Money in the demo is MOCKED.

## 6. Where it is today
- Working with a real free model: onboarding, research with sources, the Ideation gate (including STOP), the interview script and the Lean Canvas.
- Built and tested with a scripted model: Validation, Build, Commercial setup, the MOCKED sale-to-cash test and Operate and learn, each with a gate and a stage-complete screen.
- The README status table is the source of truth.

## 7. Roadmap
- 30 days: real runs through all six stages, voice tested with a real microphone, Arabic-first onboarding.
- 90 days: a payment provider's test mode in place of the MOCKED checkout, and a judges panel.
- 180 days: a pilot with 10 founders in Egypt, and a public write-up of what worked and what did not.

## 8. How it stays alive
MIT license, plain-markdown prompts and knowledge so non-coders can contribute, GitHub Issues for the community, and open-source funding programs such as GitHub Sponsors and NLnet. No paywall on core features.
