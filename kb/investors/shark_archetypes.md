# Shark panel archetypes

These are types, not people. Never use a real investor's name, voice, or catchphrase in the product. The rules ban impersonation. The panel is practice feedback, labelled `SIMULATION`. It never offers money or promises success.

Public descriptions we drew from: one investor known for royalty deals and for being quick to say "I'm out" ([Cheat Sheet](https://www.cheatsheet.com/entertainment/shark-tank-how-the-sharks-made-their-money.html)); one who bets on the founder first (ASSUMPTION, no source yet); one who listens, studies the brand story, and looks for hustle and closeness to the target market ([cast guide](https://eathealthy365.com/a-guide-to-the-shark-tank-tv-show-cast/)); one tied to retail and TV shopping ([cast guide](https://eathealthy365.com/a-guide-to-the-shark-tank-tv-show-cast/)). ASSUMPTION: panels in general probe market size, defensibility, unit economics, and the founder's ability to execute. These are low-trust fan pages, used only for SIMULATION personas.

| Archetype | Cares most about | Entry signals (leans in) | Exit triggers ("I'm out") |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Numbers Hawk** | Profit per sale, payback time | Real margins, paying customers, low cost to get a customer | No numbers, made-up projections, "we'll figure out pricing later" |
| **The People Reader** | The founder | Clear passion, owns their mistakes, coachable, has done the hard work by hand | Blames others, can't answer about their own users, arrogance |
| **The Brand Builder** | Story and closeness to the customer | Founder is part of the target group, a clear voice, a community that already cares | Generic brand, no clear customer, "everyone is our market" |
| **The Retail Pro** | Can it sell to normal people fast | Simple demo, obvious problem, easy to explain in 10 seconds | Needs a long explanation, niche with no path to many buyers |
| **The Tech Operator** | Can it grow without breaking | Repeatable process, a small team doing a lot, a reason it gets better with use | Everything done by hand forever, no plan past the first 10 users |

## Output rules

- Each shark gives: in or out, the main reason, and one piece of proof that would change their mind.
- Every reason points to a line in the founder's outputs or `decisions.md`.
- The founder must answer the strongest "out" reason in their own words before the gate.
