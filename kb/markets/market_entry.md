# How to enter any market

## The idea in one line

Win one small group of customers completely, then grow from there. Aulet calls that first group the **beachhead market**, after the Normandy landing ([summary](https://tianpan.co/blog/2025/06/14/disciplined-entrepreneurship)).

## Steps (adapted from the first steps of Disciplined Entrepreneurship)

1. **Split the market into groups.** List 5–10 possible customer groups. Do real interviews, not desk research only ([summary](https://hackernoon.com/lessons-from-disciplined-entrepreneurship-9a86a243aebc)).
2. **Pick one beachhead.** Choose the group where the founder has the most access and the pain is sharpest. Aulet suggests a beachhead worth roughly $20–100 million a year: big enough to matter, small enough to win ([summary](https://tianpan.co/blog/2025/06/14/disciplined-entrepreneurship)).
3. **Describe the end user.** Age, job, where they spend time online, what they already pay for.
4. **Size the beachhead** from the ground up: customers × price.
5. **Write one real persona.** A real person from interviews, not a made-up one.
6. **Name the next 10 customers.** Real names the founder can contact ([review](https://www.ybrikman.com/blog/2022/08/29/disciplined-entrepreneurship/)).

Then grow into the next group that is closest to the first one.

## Founder strengths first

Pick the beachhead with this score. The founder fills it in; the co-founder only questions it.

| Question | Score 0–2 |
|---|---|
| Is the founder part of this group, or close to it? | |
| Can the founder reach 10 of them this week? | |
| Does the founder have a skill this group needs? | |
| Does the founder speak their language and know their habits? | |
| Does this group already pay for a fix? | |

Highest total wins. Ties go to the group the founder can reach fastest.

## When entering a new country

Check these before picking a beachhead there. Each answer needs a source or `ASSUMPTION`.

1. **Language:** what language do buyers search and buy in?
2. **Channels:** which apps and groups do they use? (Example for Egypt: see `egypt.md`.)
3. **Payment habits:** how do they pay for digital products? Our product only plans this; all payments stay `MOCKED`.
4. **Price level:** what do similar products cost locally?
5. **Trust:** what makes buyers trust a new seller? (Reviews must be real. We never fake them.)
6. **Rules:** any local rules for the product type? We don't give legal advice; flag it for a lawyer.
