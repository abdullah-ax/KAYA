# Egypt market notes

Numbers change. The research intern re-checks each one before using it and cites the date.

## People online

- Egypt had 57.5 million social media user identities in October 2026, about 47.7% of the population ([DataReportal Digital 2027: Egypt](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-egypt)).
- The year before, DataReportal counted 98.2 million internet users in October 2025 ([Digital 2026: Egypt](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-egypt)).
- Note: "social media user identities" may not equal unique people (DataReportal's own warning).

## How people pay

- InstaPay and the Instant Payment Network passed 2.4 billion transactions worth about EGP 7 trillion through September 2026, per the Central Bank of Egypt ([Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/581997.aspx), page blocks automated checks; unverified until a second source is added).
- Cash is still a large share of e-commerce payments. One source puts cash on delivery near 40% ([Orchestra](https://www.orchestrapay.com/coverage/africa/egypt)); another puts all cash at about 31% ([8b](https://www.8b.world/countries/egypt)). Both are payment vendors. Sources disagree, so treat it as a range.
- Fawry offers online, wallet, and cash payment through a large network of retail points ([8b](https://www.8b.world/countries/egypt)).
- Common methods: cash, mobile wallets (Vodafone Cash and others), Meeza cards, Fawry ([Shuttle](https://www.shuttleglobal.com/blog/the-most-popular-payment-methods-in-egypt/)).

Our product never processes payments. These notes help the founder plan pricing and checkout. All payment steps stay `MOCKED`.

## What this means for a digital product (ASSUMPTION until tested)

- Offer Arabic first if the buyer is local. Test English for regional or global buyers.
- WhatsApp and Facebook groups are likely first channels. Confirm with the founder's own groups.
- Price in EGP for local buyers. Compare against 3 local products with links.
- Plan a payment path the buyer already uses (wallet or InstaPay transfer) before cards.

## Global vs. Egypt

Start where the founder has access. A founder in Cairo with an Arabic-speaking audience should usually test locally first, then move to the closest next market (other Arabic-speaking countries, or Egyptians abroad). That's the beachhead idea in `market_entry.md`. This is our ASSUMPTION; the founder decides.
