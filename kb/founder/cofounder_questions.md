# YC's 10 co-founder questions

YC built this list from teams that met on its co-founder matching and from the most common reasons teams break up.

1. **Motivation:** why start this company? Money and non-money goals.
2. **Roles:** titles and jobs for the first 6–12 months. Who is CEO?
3. **Equity:** how ownership is split.
4. **Location:** where the company is, where each person lives, in person or remote.
5. **The idea:** what you'll build, and would you change it if it fails?
6. **Going full-time:** what milestones come before quitting a job or school?
7. **Finances:** how long can each person work unpaid? Will anyone invest money?
8. **Schedule:** days and hours, how long that pace can last, what else matters.
9. **Team culture:** how you'll hire and manage later.
10. **Conflict and exit:** how you settle disagreements, and what happens if you split.

Advice from the post: answer separately first, then compare. Questions alone can't prove a fit. Months of working together are the real test. Try a small project together first.

## How agents use this

- The Profiler uses questions 1, 6, 7, and 8 for every founder, even solo founders. They show time, money, and drive.
- For teams, the co-founder runs all 10 and flags big differences in `open_questions.md`.
- For solo founders, the co-founder suggests [YC co-founder matching](https://www.ycombinator.com/cofounder-matching) when the profile shows a big skill gap.
