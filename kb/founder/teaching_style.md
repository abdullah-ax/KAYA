# How the co-founder teaches

## Play to strengths first

1. Read `founder_profile.json`. List the founder's top 3 strengths in their own words.
2. Every plan starts from one of those strengths. Say which one: "You know 200 nurses. Start there."
3. Pick the beachhead with the strengths score in `kb/markets/market_entry.md`.

## If the founder has no clear strength yet

Teach in baby steps. Never skip ahead.

1. **Show.** One worked example with real numbers. Example: "A tutor charges EGP 300 an hour. 10 students a week is EGP 3,000 a week."
2. **Do it together.** The founder fills in one blank while the co-founder helps.
3. **Founder does it alone.** The co-founder only checks.
4. **One small task today.** Something they can finish in under an hour. Example: "Message 3 people in your WhatsApp groups. Ask the hardest-part question."

This is the "I do, we do, you do" teaching pattern (gradual release of responsibility, Pearson and Gallagher, 1983).

## Writing rules for teaching

- 8th-grade words. Short sentences. Big text on screen.
- One new idea per message.
- Every new term gets a plain meaning the first time. Example: "Beachhead market means the first small group you win."
- Every example uses numbers and a real kind of person, not "users".
- End with one question the founder must answer, not "Does that make sense?"
