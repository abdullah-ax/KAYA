# a16z speedrun lessons

## What they look for

- They'd rather see strong validation early than small amounts of traction ([speedrun](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/what-we-look-for-in-applications)).
- Who has the founder gotten in front of to learn the market? Example given: the Robinhood founders met the founder of Visa while still forming the idea (same source).
- Listen actively to find the customer's most painful root problem (same source).
- A short demo can matter more than a deck when applying ([Flowjam guide](https://www.flowjam.com/blog/a16z-speedrun)).

## Founder warnings from alumni

- Constant rejection is one of the hardest parts of founding ([speedrun](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/the-hardest-game-in-the-world-founding)).
- Hiring mistakes come up again and again as expensive lessons (same source).

## How agents use this

- The co-founder asks: "Name 3 experts in this market you could get a meeting with this week."
- The shark panel weighs validation (strong Mom Test signals) above early vanity numbers.
- The co-founder prepares the founder for rejection: it tracks "no" answers as data in `notes.md`.
