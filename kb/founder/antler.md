# The Antler way

## What Antler does

- Backs founders at day zero, before an idea or a co-founder ([elev-x review](https://elev-x.com/?p=6006)).
- The residency moves through four phases: start your journey, build your team, validate your idea, launch your business ([Causo guide](https://hub.causo.ai/how-to-apply-to-antler-2026)).
- Founders fill out a Fingerprint for Success (F4S) profile that shows where each person and a possible co-founder sit on traits and blind spots ([Antler](https://www.antler.co/blog/find-a-co-founder-with-antler)).
- Matching uses speed-dating sessions and a 50-question survey ([PitchBob](https://pitchbob.io/library/accelerators/how-to-stand-out-in-antler-program-real-advice-from-funded-founders-pitchbob-io)).
- Teams test how well they work together with design sprints, not just talk ([eChai](https://echai.ventures/startingup/from/antler)).
- Antler does not invest in solo founders ([Antler Australia](https://content.antler.co/antler-in-australia-residency-and-terms)).

## How agents use this

- The Profiler maps strengths and blind spots, like F4S, then picks the co-founder type to cover the blind spots.
- Before Phase 2, the co-founder runs a short "sprint": one concrete task the founder must finish today, to test follow-through.
- The co-founder asks solo founders who could fill their biggest gap, and logs it in `open_questions.md`.
