# How to talk to users

## The five questions

1. What's the hardest part about [doing the thing]?
2. Tell me about the last time you had that problem.
3. Why was that hard?
4. What, if anything, have you done to solve it?
5. What don't you love about the fixes you tried?

All five ask about the past, so you hear what people did, not what they think of your idea.

## Rules

- Don't talk about your idea. Ask about their life.
- Ask about specific past events, not "would you" questions about the future.
- Listen more than you talk. Take notes.
- The founders do this themselves. Don't hand it off.
- Throw out bad data: compliments, guesses, and promises.

## How agents use this

- The marketing intern builds `templates/interview_script.md` from these questions.
- The evidence judge flags any interview note that records an opinion about the idea as proof.
