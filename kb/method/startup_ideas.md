# How to get startup ideas

## Main point

Don't try to think up ideas. Look for problems, preferably ones you have yourself.

## Related essays

- [Do Things That Don't Scale](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)
- [The 18 Mistakes That Kill Startups](https://paulgraham.com/startupmistakes.html): the biggest one is not making something users want.
- [Default Alive or Default Dead?](https://paulgraham.com/aord.html)
- [All essays, YC collection](https://ycombinator.com/library/carousel/Essays%20by%20Paul%20Graham)

## How agents use this

- The Profiler asks: "What problem have you had yourself in the last month?" and "What do people in your groups complain about?"
- The research intern ranks ideas higher when the founder has lived the problem. That's part of founder-market fit.
- The co-founder pushes back on ideas that start from a technology instead of a problem.
