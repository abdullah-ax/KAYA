# Market sizing

## Method

1. **Count from the ground up first:** number of customers who could buy × price per year. Show where each number comes from.
2. **Check from the top down:** industry totals from a cited report, cut to your region and segment.
3. **Obtainable share:** a small, defended slice for years 1–3. Explain why that slice.
4. Every number gets a link or `ASSUMPTION`.

## Red flags

- "Serviceable market" equals "total market": you haven't thought about who actually buys ([skills.sh market-research note](https://www.skills.sh/aaaaqwq/claude-code-skills/market-research)).
- A share number with no reason behind it.
- A single source for every number.
