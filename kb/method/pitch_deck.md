# Pitch deck structure

## YC seed deck

Aaron Harris wrote YC's seed deck template to show founders how to lay out their story in slides ([YC library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/2u-how-to-build-your-seed-round-pitch-deck)). Read the source for the exact slide order before building the template. Our working order, to check against it: title, problem, solution, traction, insight, business model, market, competition, team, ask.

## Airbnb 2008 deck

Often used as the model of a simple, clear deck ([SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/airbnb-pitch-deck-from-2008/65585661)). Our working list of its slides, to check against the source: welcome, problem, solution, market validation, market size, product, business model, market adoption, competition, competitive advantages, team.

## Rules for our decks

- One idea per slide. 15 words at most. Big type.
- Each slide names the real slide it copies the layout of.
- No AI images. No gradients.
- Every number has a source on the slide or in the speaker notes.
- Pass `slop_check.py` and `npx impeccable detect`.
