# Do things that don't scale

## Main points

- Startups take off because the founders make them take off. Most startups are fragile at first.
- Recruit the first users by hand, one by one. Don't wait for them to find you.
- A small group of users who love the product beats a big group who like it.
- Give early users far more personal attention than seems sensible.
- Do the work by hand first if needed, before automating it.

Summary sources: [YC library](https://ycombinator.com/library/carousel/Essays%20by%20Paul%20Graham), [summary](https://echai.ventures/startingup/from/paulgraham-com).

## How agents use this

- The marketing intern's first plan is always a list of 10 named people or places the founder can reach by hand this week. Ads come later.
- The co-founder asks: "Who are the first 10 people? Write their names."
- The Phase 5 plan includes a manual onboarding step for the first users.
