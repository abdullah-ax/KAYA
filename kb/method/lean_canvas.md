# Lean Canvas

One page, nine boxes. Fill it in this order, and mark every box `ASSUMPTION` until there's proof.

1. **Customer segments:** who exactly. Name the early adopters.
2. **Problem:** top 1–3 problems, plus how they solve them today.
3. **Unique value proposition:** one sentence on why you're different and worth attention.
4. **Solution:** the smallest feature set for each problem.
5. **Channels:** how you reach customers. Start with ones you can do by hand.
6. **Revenue streams:** price and how you charge.
7. **Cost structure:** fixed and per-customer costs.
8. **Key metrics:** the 1–3 numbers that show it's working.
9. **Unfair advantage:** something that can't be easily copied or bought. Often comes from the founder profile.

## How agents use this

- `templates/lean_canvas.md` has the nine boxes. Each box needs a source line or `ASSUMPTION`.
- The founder writes the unfair advantage box. The co-founder can only suggest, using lines from `founder_profile.json`.
- The Phase 2 gate counts how many boxes have real evidence.
