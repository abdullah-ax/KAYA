# The Mom Test

The name: your mom will say your idea is great to be nice. Good questions get useful answers even from people who want to be nice.

## Three rules

1. Talk about their life, not your idea.
2. Ask about specific things that happened in the past, not opinions or guesses about the future.
3. Talk less, listen more.

## Signals

- **Weak signals:** compliments ("I'd use that"), general claims ("I always…"), future promises ("I'd pay for that").
- **Strong signals:** they already spent money or time on the problem; they give you their time, an intro, or a pre-order.

## How agents use this

- The interview script never asks "Would you use this?"
- The evidence judge marks any claim built only on compliments or future promises as weak.
- At the Phase 2 gate, only strong signals count toward the target.
