# MVP and launch

## Main points

- An MVP should be a little embarrassing. Ship it fast ([Startup School notes](https://medium.com/@davegoldblatt/startup-school-notes-orientation-fbe4201b977c), a secondary write-up that blocks automated checks; low trust on its own).
- Launch more than once. Each new feature or customer is a reason to launch again.
- Code and talking to users are the two core jobs ([talk notes](https://lindatong.substack.com/p/takeaways-from-ycs-startup-school)).
- Months without a release usually means the startup is in trouble ([summary](https://hackernoon.com/the-10-things-ive-learned-attending-y-combinator-startup-school-2020-bs2g3ygr)).

## How agents use this

- The builder's spec covers the smallest version that answers one question from the founder's interviews.
- The builder cuts any feature that no user story or interview note asks for.
- The co-founder plans a first small launch to the 10 named people before any public post.
