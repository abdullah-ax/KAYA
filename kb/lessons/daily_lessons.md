# Daily lessons

The co-founder picks one lesson per day for the founder's weakest area. Each one is under about 20 minutes. Each comes with one action due today.

| Weak spot | Lesson | Source | Today's action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Talks about the idea instead of listening | How to Talk to Users (Gustaf Alströmer) | [Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/aspiring-founders) | Run one interview with the 5 questions; paste notes |
| Starts from a technology, not a problem | How to Get Startup Ideas | [Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html) | List 5 problems you had in the last month |
| Waits for users to come | Do Things That Don't Scale | [Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html) | Write the names of 10 people you can reach today |
| Overbuilds | How to Build an MVP (Michael Seibel) | [Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/aspiring-founders) | Cut your feature list to 3 |
| Afraid to launch | How to Launch (Again and Again) (Kat Manalac) | [Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/aspiring-founders) | Send the product to 3 people |
| No customers yet | How to Get Your First Customers (Gustaf Alstromer) | [Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/aspiring-founders) | Ask 3 people for a pre-order or a meeting |
| Unsure about a co-founder | 10 Questions to Discuss with a Potential Co-founder | [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/10-questions-to-discuss-with-a-potential-co-founder) | Answer the 10 questions on your own |
| Team friction | How to Work Together (Kevin Hale) | [Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/aspiring-founders) | Agree on one weekly 1:1 slot |
| Ignores the money | Default Alive or Default Dead? | [Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/aord.html) | Write your monthly costs and months of runway |
| Making common mistakes | The 18 Mistakes That Kill Startups | [Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/startupmistakes.html) | Pick the one mistake you're closest to and write a fix |
| Weak pitch | How to Build Your Seed Round Pitch Deck (Aaron Harris) | [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/2u-how-to-build-your-seed-round-pitch-deck) | Write your problem slide in 15 words |

Rule: never summarize a lesson as if it were the full source. Link to it and give one action.
