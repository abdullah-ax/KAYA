# Interview script (Phase 1, step 3)

Source: `kb/method/talk_to_users.md`, `kb/method/mom_test.md`.

**Before:** don't mention your idea. Ask about their life. Take notes.

1. What's the hardest part about [the thing]?
2. Tell me about the last time that happened.
3. Why was that hard?
4. What have you done to fix it?
5. What don't you love about what you tried?

**Close:** Who else should I talk to? Can I follow up next week?
