# Product

## Register

product

## Users

First-time founders, often students or early-career people in Egypt and similar markets, with 5 to 20 hours a week and little or no money to spend. They use Boardroom on a laptop in the evening, in short focused sessions, sometimes by voice. Their job: get from "who am I and what could I build" to a tested, sellable digital product without fooling themselves.

## Product Purpose

An open-source team of AI agents that coaches the founder through six gated stages: Ideation, Validation, Build, Pricing and setup, Distribution, Operate and learn. The founder makes every decision in their own words. The system shows its sources, labels assumptions, and recommends stop when proof is weak. Success is a founder who either has paying strangers or stopped early and cheaply, and learned something real either way.

## Brand Personality

Honest, calm, demanding. A warm skeptic: kind in tone, strict on evidence. Plain words at an 8th-grade reading level. Every sentence carries a fact, a number, a name, or a decision. Light humor lives in the mascots, never in the evidence.

## Anti-references

- Hype startup tools: purple-to-blue gradients, glass cards, "10x your idea" copy, confetti on every click.
- Chat-only AI wrappers where the bot decides and the user clicks "yes".
- Dense enterprise dashboards with hero metrics and identical card grids.
- Gamified streak guilt (Duolingo's shaming, countdown timers).

## Design Principles

1. The founder decides. No screen lets a bare "yes" move work forward.
2. Show the proof. Every claim sits next to its source, trust level, or the word ASSUMPTION.
3. Always know where you are. The map of stages, agents and open issues is one click away from anywhere.
4. Say stop plainly. Weak results are shown in red, never softened.
5. Learn while doing. Every step offers one short, linked lesson and a real case study.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text 18px, nothing under 16px except dense metadata at 13–14px. Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text. Every action reachable by keyboard with a visible focus ring. Reduced motion respected: mascot and face animations fall back to still images. English UI with Arabic text supported later; layout must not assume left-to-right only.
