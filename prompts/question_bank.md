# Question bank

The Profiler picks from these. One at a time. Skip anything the brief already answers. Plain words.

## Problems you know

- What problem did you have yourself in the last month? What did you do about it?
- What do people in your groups complain about most?
- What do people ask you for help with?

## Access

- Which groups are you part of? (Clubs, schools, jobs, WhatsApp groups, online communities, family business.) About how many people in each?
- Who could you message today who would reply within a day?
- Who trusts your opinion on something? On what?
- What languages do you speak well enough to sell in?

## Skills

- What have you built, sold, taught, or organized before? What happened?
- What would friends say you're best at?
- What skill do you wish you had for this?

## Time, money, risk

- How many hours a week can you give this for the next 3 months?
- How much money can you spend without hurting yourself? (Can be zero.)
- How long can you work on this unpaid?
- What milestone would make you go full-time?

## Values

- What kind of business would you never run?
- Who do you want to help, and why them?

## Personality

- When a plan goes wrong, what do you usually do first?
- Do you prefer to plan everything, or start and fix as you go? Give an example.
- When someone disagrees with you, what do you do?
- Last time you started something, how far did you get? Why did it stop?

Sources: [YC co-founder questions](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/10-questions-to-discuss-with-a-potential-co-founder), [Paul Graham: How to Get Startup Ideas](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html).
