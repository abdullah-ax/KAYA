# Founder brief: chicks to Bentley

I'm 24, I live in Mansoura, and I have 2,500 EGP saved. My plan: buy 50 chicks.
They grow up and lay eggs. The eggs hatch into more chicks. Those chicks grow up
and lay more eggs. I keep going, and in two years I'll have enough chickens to buy
a Bentley. My uncle did something like this in the village, so I know it works.
I can give it 20 hours a week.
