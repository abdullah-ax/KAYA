# Founder brief: Mariam, IELTS speaking coach

## Idea
A digital practice pack for the IELTS speaking test. Students record answers to real-style questions at home. They get a short checklist and a model answer to compare with.

## Skills
- I have coached IELTS speaking for 4 years. I have coached about 300 students since 2021.
- I scored band 8.5 in speaking myself.
- I make short practice videos for my students on my phone.

## Access
- I run a WhatsApp group of 420 IELTS students in Alexandria. Most sit the test within 6 months.
- 2 language centres in Smouha send me students each month.

## Time and money
- 10 hours a week for the next 3 months.
- I can spend EGP 2,000 without hurting myself.

## Target market
IELTS test takers in Egypt who need band 6.5 or 7 for university or work abroad.

## Values
- I only quote real students, and only if they agree.
- I will not promise a band score.
